# Sqafe

[Sqafe](https://sqafe.com/) is a QR code inspection site. Its SafeScan QR scanner decodes QR content and shows available risk signals before someone decides whether to open a destination.

The scanner can read a camera capture, an uploaded QR image, or pasted decoded content. For supported links it attempts destination, redirect, and configured reputation checks; missing required checks are shown as unverified. A scan describes signals at that moment and does not certify a link, business, or payment recipient as safe.
