The project

About Sqafe

Sqafe is a QR code inspection site. Its SafeScan QR scanner lets you scan, upload, or paste a QR payload to review what it contains before you open a destination.

The name Sqafe identifies this website. SafeScan QR is the scanner used here.

Open the scanner

What a scan tells you

SafeScan QR decodes the content and, for supported links, attempts destination, redirect, and configured reputation checks. It shows when a required check could not complete.

A result describes the signals available at scan time. A destination or QR code can change, and a scan cannot confirm who placed a physical code or who will receive a payment. Confirm important requests through a trusted source before acting.

Read the QR safety guide for practical checks, or contact Sqafe with a question or security report.

Public project

The SafeScan QR repository on GitHub contains the website and scanner code. The public project name and domain are Sqafe and sqafe.com.
