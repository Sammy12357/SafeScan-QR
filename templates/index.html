Can you distinguish a malicious QR?

Scan once, inspect the hidden payload, and safely continue without rescanning. This Python-backed demo checks QR destinations with Google Safe Browsing before anything runs.

Scan QR Airdrop
Demo focus
  • Decodes QR directly via Python ZBar
  • Real-time Google Safe Browsing API checks
  • Database caching for 24-hour safety windows
  • Tracks scan progress for airdrop tiers

Why this problem matters

Most people cannot tell the difference between a malicious QR code and a safe one before scanning. SafeScan QR reveals the hidden payload first, so unsafe links, payment requests, and app actions are easier to spot.

What makes this different?

Instead of immediately opening or executing a QR action, SafeScan QR explains what the payload wants to do and why it looks safe or suspicious.

How does scanning work?

The backend decodes uploaded QR images in Python, checks the destination, caches results, and stores user scan progress.

What is the hackathon hook?

It combines a clear cybersecurity problem, real authentication, a Python safety engine, and token-gated community rewards.
