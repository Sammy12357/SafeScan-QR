Use this portal to submit GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA, or general privacy requests. SafeScan logs the request timestamp and targets completion within 30 days.

GDPR Rights

EU/EEA users may request access, erasure, rectification, restriction, portability, or object to legitimate-interest processing.

California Privacy Rights

California residents may request to know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing, limit sensitive personal information, and receive equal service after opting out.

LGPD and PIPEDA

Brazilian and Canadian users can access, correct, challenge, export, delete, and revoke consent using the same request handlers.
