One request to registered data brokers

California's free DROP service lets an eligible resident submit a deletion request to registered data brokers. The state verifies residency and shows each broker's response.

For a family member, follow DROP's rules for helping another resident submit their own request.

Enter details on California's site. SafeScan does not send your saved Go Ghost profile or collect your DROP ID.
