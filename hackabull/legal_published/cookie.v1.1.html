With a fresh “Accept All” choice, the scanner may send first-party performance counts. The event body omits QR text, images, and device IDs, but the network request may still carry transport metadata such as an IP address. Global Privacy Control suppresses these optional counts even if a previous choice allowed them; a Sec-GPC: 1 request also prevents the server from recording an “all” choice. Applicability to sale or sharing rights and provider behavior needs counsel and production review.

Older versions may have left safeScanGoGhostProfile, safeScanGoGhostProgress, or safeScanGoGhostConsent in local storage. Current Go Ghost code removes these legacy keys when it runs; they are not active storage keys. Provider-set cookies and browser cache are not covered by the first-party table.
